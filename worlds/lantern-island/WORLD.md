# Lantern Island

A storm has cut the island ferry route. At dusk the lighthouse sends a signal from a room locked for twenty years. The player arrives as the temporary keeper with a single key and a damaged logbook.

The village depends on three places: the harbor cooperative, the cliff archive, and the lighthouse. Keeper Mira wants to repair the light before midnight; archivist Rowan insists the signal is a warning; ferry mechanic Jun has found fresh rope inside the sealed room. Each has useful knowledge and an incomplete account, rather than a predetermined villain role.

Salt water corrodes every mechanism, and reliable electricity lasts only a few hours. There is no combat requirement. Investigation, negotiation and practical repairs should all provide ways forward. Establish clues in the story before asking the player to infer an answer. Preserve player choices and avoid deciding their emotions or actions.

At the opening, the warning bell rings while Mira asks for help at the generator and Rowan offers to open the archive. Let the player choose where to go or propose another course. The missing page in the logbook links the current signal to a rescue that was never recorded.
