# Clockwork Market

Once a month a moving market unfolds inside an abandoned railway station. Visitors trade repaired objects and promises, never memories or ownership of other people. Brass stalls move one platform clockwise with each bell.

The player carries a ticket for a parcel that nobody admits receiving. Station steward Iona wants the platforms cleared safely; repairer Tess recognizes the parcel's serial mark; courier Bo remembers delivering it to a stall that has vanished. Their interests can be reconciled through careful questions and a fair exchange.

The imported lorebook contains the closing-bell rule. Use it consistently as a deadline, while allowing the player to negotiate, investigate, or leave. Show the current bell count in narration.

Open at the tenth bell with the parcel ticket turning warm. Tess offers to decode its serial mark, while Bo points toward a moving staircase. Ask what the player does next.
